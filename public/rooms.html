Rooms & fee guide

Use these fields to keep room assignments and monthly payment records consistent.

▤

Room assignment

Assign a room number during resident registration. Keep room labels consistent, such as A-101 or B-204.

₹

Fee tracking

Set each resident’s monthly fee and mark their payment status as Paid, Pending or Partially Paid.

⇄

Check-in / check-out

Use the stay status and date fields to record arrivals, reservations and departures.

Recommended resident workflow

  1. Choose Insert resident and fill in the resident’s name, room, contact and fee details.
  2. Use Resident register to review all records and search for a resident.
  3. Choose Update details to change room assignments, dates or fee status.
  4. When a resident leaves, update their stay status to Checked out and enter a check-out date.
  5. Use Remove resident only when the record should be permanently deleted.

Note: This page is a management guide. Room availability and payment processing are not automated in this starter version.
